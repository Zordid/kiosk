KIOSK

Order management for the Quickbox kiosks by Olaf Gottschalk, 1991–1993. Running here in DOSBox, in your browser. The full story, and its Windows successor WAWIS, are in the repository.

Kiosk 1.2C (November 1993)

Turbo Pascal 6.0 with Turbo Vision, German, built in 2026 from the original source with reconstructed units. You are at the headquarters in Barsinghausen, and a kiosk phones in its order.

  • Enter closes the about box, Esc the list of current orders
  • F4 takes an order: Enter picks the customer, then type the line number on the order form, Enter, the quantity (Tab for a remark), Enter, and the next number; F2 saves the order
  • F8 current orders: Enter shows one, Alt+D prints it (there is no printer here)
  • Alt+S master data: A articles, K customers, L wholesalers; type to search, Ins adds an entry
  • Menus with Alt+the red letter, or the mouse; Alt+X ends, KIOSK restarts
Start Kiosk 1.2C

Click into the screen to type. On a Mac, the function keys may need fn. The DOS clock is set to 26 November 1993, the day after 1.2C was finished. The articles are those of 1993; the two customers, "Puffreis, Pit" with his order of 1992 and "Salmiak, Susi", are made up (the kiosks' real owners are not shown), and so are all phone numbers. Whatever you change stays in your browser only. How the order editor came about is told in the story.
